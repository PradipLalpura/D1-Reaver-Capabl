Pipeline

01 Compile

Your sentence becomes a structured spec — geography, industry, size, signals, testable criteria. Optional business context sharpens buyer-fit.

02 Discover

Search mesh with ordered fallbacks. Pools grow to 2.5× asked; research waves loop until the count fills or sources honestly exhaust.

03 Research

Robots-gated fetches, cached pages, RAG passages with embeddings — every PASS cites its sources.

04 Qualify

Code-graded PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN. Conflicts and stale facts become UNCERTAIN, never averaged into fake certainty.

05 Deliver

Qualified plus capped uncertain leads ship with per-lead reasons; disqualified land in a rejected appendix. CSV by default.

06 Refresh

Reruns reuse fresh evidence and refetch stale pages — REFRESHED, FRESH, STALE, or GONE, each explained.
