The strike sequence

Six cuts.
No wasted motion.

I. Compile

Your sentence becomes a battle plan — geography, industry, size, signals, testable criteria. Business context sharpens buyer-fit.

II. Discover

Eleven routed sources with fallbacks. Pools swell to 2.5× asked; research waves loop until the count fills or sources run dry.

III. Research

Robots-gated fetches, cached pages, embedded passages. Every PASS cites the exact lines that earned it.

IV. Qualify

Code-graded PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN. Conflicts and rot become UNCERTAIN — never averaged into false confidence.

V. Deliver

Qualified plus capped uncertain ship with per-lead reasons. The disqualified are listed with their wounds, never counted.

VI. Refresh

Reruns reuse fresh evidence and re-cut stale pages — REFRESHED, FRESH, STALE, or GONE, each explained.
