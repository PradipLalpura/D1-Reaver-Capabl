Pipeline

1 · Compile

Your sentence becomes a structured target spec — geography, industry, size, signals, testable criteria. Optional business context sharpens buyer-fit.

2 · Discover

Multi-backend search mesh with ordered fallbacks. Pools grow to 2.5× asked; research runs in waves until the count fills.

3 · Research

Pages fetched through a robots.txt gate, cached, chunked into an evidence store with embeddings for citation retrieval.

4 · Qualify

Code-graded PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN per criterion. Conflicts and stale facts become UNCERTAIN — never averaged into fake certainty.

5 · Deliver

Qualified plus capped uncertain leads ship with per-lead reasons; disqualified go to a rejected appendix with their FAIL reasons. CSV by default.

6 · Refresh

Reruns reuse fresh evidence and refetch stale pages — REFRESHED, FRESH, STALE, or GONE, each explained.
